About Mason Armijo

Background

My name is Mason Armijo, I am 26 and originally from Allen, Texas. After graduating High Schhool in 2018, I enlisted in the United States Army as a 25U (Signal Operations Support Specialist). After completing my training I was assigned to Fort Carson, Colorado to the 71st EOD (Explosive Ordnance Disposal) headquarters, and served as the lead communications specialist. After completing five years of Active Duty, I continued my time in the Army Reserves to focus on my career outside of the Army. I have always enjoyed computers and aerospace and decided to continue working in the field as I transitioned into civilian life. In doing so I have gained many noteable skills and great experience from my first position as a Protected Band Watch Officer. In this position i was responsible for being the 24/7 point of contact for real time awareness of the AEHF/MILSTAR constellations for the United States Space Force and Air Force. I was responsible for reportin any incidents or possible attacks on the constellations within a one hour window. I posted user message on the SIPR Network for all military departments to ensure all data was secured and accessible in case of emergencies such as jamming, and cyber attacks. In my current position as a COMSEC Responsible Officer, I am responsible for ensuring the security and integrity of the communication systems and data for my company; this includes but is not limited to encryptors, both on the ground and in space, COMSEC keys, radios, as well as classified systems. I work closely with and report to the NSA, and am responsible for creating, editing, and submitting all documentation to them. I ensure all NSA policies and procedures are enforced and followed at my company, as well as give quarterly trainigs on them. In July 2026, I completed eight years of honorable service, and decided to pivot focus to my professional and academic career. I started my first year at MSU Denver in the Fall of 2026, and am currently pursuing a Bachelors Degree in Cybersecurity.
